Profile posts

Archive the full post history of any profile you can view on X — original posts, reposts, and replies. The exporter targets the profile open in the tab you launched it from, or the last profile captured request was for (loading its Posts tab is enough; loading the Replies tab additionally includes replies). Even simpler: visit the profile, open the toolbar popup, and click Download archive — collection and export happen automatically.
